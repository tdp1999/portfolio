Private content renders here, and only here.

signed in · under the header

Private pages form one group. A new private page is one entry in PRIVATE_ROUTES and one in workspacePages. The whole area lives in pages/private/ (routes, frame, sign-in page). The group gives each page the rest: the sign-in redirect (nothing private is created before sign-in), the workspace bar, an HttpClient that sends the token on API calls, RenderMode.Client and X-Robots-Tag: noindex. The feature itself holds no auth code.

The session is app-wide state, restored once per page load. Signed out, the header shows a "Sign in" item between Contact and More; it opens /sign-in, a feature page like /contact with the form (no heading of its own) centred under the page title. A private page opened while signed out sends the Owner there with ?next= and comes back after. Signed in, the item becomes the Workspace menu, listing the private pages from workspacePages.

On a private page, landing-workspace-bar sits right under the header: the same pages as tabs, the owner badge and sign-out. Public pages never show it. Sign-out goes home first, so the header is back to "Sign in" with nothing private on screen. The account is the console's, and so is the session: prod cookies are scoped to the parent domain, so signing in or out here does the same on the console.
